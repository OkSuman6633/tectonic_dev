## 1. What Tectonic is

Tectonic lets people **build their own AI assistants ("agents")** by simply describing what they
want in plain English. They can then connect those assistants to their data (like Google Analytics
or Meta Ads) and let them do real work.

We charge for **three things**, and we keep them separate so the price always feels fair:

| What we charge for | In everyday words |
|---|---|
| **AI usage** | How much the AI "thinks and writes" for you — like a data plan for the AI |
| **Capabilities** | Which tools and powers are unlocked (memory, data connections, images, etc.) |
| **Trust & controls** | Security, team permissions, audit history — the things businesses need |

A simple rule guides everything: **"You can always see what's available; you only pay more when you
want more."**

---

## 2. How our pricing works — three simple ideas

**Idea 1 — A monthly allowance.** Every plan includes a monthly amount of AI usage. Think of it like
a mobile data plan: use what you need, and we'll tell you clearly when you're getting close to the
limit.

**Idea 2 — Unlocking features.** Some powerful features (long-term memory, team accounts, extra data
sources) are only available on higher plans. If a feature is locked, you'll see a small lock icon and
an "Upgrade" button — it's never hidden or surprising.

**Idea 3 — A friendly limit, not a wall.** If you hit your monthly limit, the product doesn't just
stop working. It tells you politely and gives you choices: buy a little more, or upgrade to a bigger
plan.

## 4. Each plan in plain words

### Starter — "Try it out"

**Who it's for:** someone curious about building their first AI assistant.

**What you get:**
- One simple assistant, built from a plain-English description.
- Basic (fast, affordable) AI models.
- A small monthly AI allowance — enough to explore, not enough to run a business on.

**What happens at the limit:** the AI pauses and shows a friendly message: *"You've reached your
monthly allowance."* with buttons to **Upgrade** or **Wait until next month**.

### Pro — "The serious individual builder"

**Who it's for:** freelancers, consultants, and power users who build assistants for themselves.

**What you get:**
- Everything in Starter, plus:
- **All AI models**, including the smartest (premium) ones.
- **Use your own AI key** if you already pay a provider (like Anthropic) — you bring the key, we do
  the rest.
- **Long-term memory** — your assistant remembers things between conversations.
- **Data connections** — plug in Google Analytics, Meta Ads, or web data.
- **Image generation** — your assistant can create images.
- A **bigger allowance** and a **top-up** option if you need more in a busy month.

### Business — "For teams that work together"

**Who it's for:** small-to-mid-size teams that share assistants and need oversight.

**What you get:**
- Everything in Pro, plus:
- **One pooled allowance** for the whole team — simpler than per-person billing.
- **Seats** for team members, with **roles and permissions** (who can build, who can view).
- **Shared team knowledge** — one place where the team's assistants learn from.
- **Human approval steps** — a person can review and approve the assistant's important actions.
- **Priority handling** — your team's work jumps the queue at busy times.
- **Better reports** — see spend and activity by person, plus exportable audit history.

### Enterprise — "Built around your company"

**Who it's for:** large or regulated organizations with specific security and scale needs.

**What you get:**
- Everything in Business, plus:
- **Custom limits** — we agree the numbers together, and they're written into your contract.
- **Single sign-on (SSO)** — your employees log in with your company login.
- **Private deployment** — run Tectonic inside your own environment if required.
- **Dedicated capacity** — your work never waits behind other customers.
- **Longest retention and full audit export** — for compliance and legal needs.
- **Custom data connections and AI endpoints** — bring your own infrastructure.

**Every card shows the same five things, in the same order**, so comparing is effortless:

2. **The headline benefit** (the one thing that matters most).
3. **A short bullet list** (3–5 plain-English items, no jargon).
4. **The allowance** shown as a simple meter (e.g., *"5M AI units/month"*) — never a scary raw

5. **One clear button** — Start, Choose, or Contact Us.

A **"Compare all plans"** link opens a friendly side-by-side table (Section 6) for people who want
detail.

### Step 2 — The plan follows the person everywhere

Once chosen, the plan isn't just a billing detail — it shapes the product experience:

| Where | What the customer sees |
|---|---|
| **Model picker** (top-right of the Studio) | Locked models are greyed out with a small 🔒 and "Upgrade to Pro". |
| **Tools & data sources** window | Locked connectors show "Available on Pro" instead of a connect button. |
| **Building an agent** | If their plan allows 1 agent, building a second shows "You've used all your agent slots". |
| **Running the assistant** | A subtle usage bar shows "used 6 of 10 this month" — visible but not nagging. |

### Step 3 — Hitting a limit is friendly, never a dead end

When the customer approaches or reaches a limit, the product explains itself in plain language and
offers a choice:

- **At 80%:** a gentle notice — *"You've used 80% of your monthly allowance."*
- **At 100%:** a clear message with three buttons:
  - **Buy more** (top-up, if the plan allows it),
  - **Upgrade** (to the next plan),
  - **Wait until next month** (their data is safe; nothing is deleted).

The same pattern is used everywhere: a lock icon for a *feature* limit, a meter for a *usage* limit,
and an "Upgrade" path always one click away. The message is always: **"You're not stuck — you've
simply reached a boundary."**

## 7. What happens when you run out (per plan, in plain words)

| **Enterprise** | The customer **never** gets hard-blocked — limits are agreed in the contract. |

## 8. Questions business people usually ask

**Q: Will a customer get a surprise bill?**
No. The allowance is set at sign-up and shown as a meter in the product. Going over requires the
customer to choose to top up or upgrade — nothing is charged silently.

**Q: What if a customer uses their own AI key (BYOK)?**
They still get the same plan experience and limits. Bringing their own key mainly affects *who pays*
the AI provider — it never removes our safeguards.

**Q: How do we protect our margins?**
The smartest AI models cost us the most, so they're only included from Pro upward. The allowance is
also capped per run, so a single runaway task can't blow through a whole month's budget.

**Q: Can Enterprise customers get special terms?**
Yes. Every Enterprise limit can be adjusted per customer and written into their contract — no code
changes needed on our side.

**Q: When do we finalize the numbers?**
The current numbers are **proposed defaults**. We'll tune them after cost modeling so each plan is
profitable at its price point.
