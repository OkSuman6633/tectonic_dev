# Tectonic Subscription Plans

Tectonic lets people build and run their own AI assistants. Each plan sets a clear monthly allowance and a set of capabilities. Below is what each plan includes and the exact limits that apply.

---

## Plans at a glance

| | **Starter** | **Pro** | **Business** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Best for | Trying it out | Individual builders | Teams | Large companies |
| Monthly AI allowance | 500K tokens | 5M tokens | 50M tokens (shared by the team) | Negotiated |
| AI assistants (agents) | 1 | 10 | 500 | Unlimited |
| AI models | Basic | All, including premium | All, including early access | All + custom |
| Price position | Entry | Mid | High | Custom |

> **Tokens** are simply how AI usage is measured — roughly, words. The monthly allowance is the amount of AI work included in the plan.

## How limits behave

- **Monthly allowance** — the total AI usage included each month. When it's nearly used, a clear notice appears (at 80% and 100%).
- **Per-run cap** — the maximum AI usage for one request, so a single large task can't consume the whole month at once.
- **Reaching a limit** — on Starter, the assistant pauses and suggests an upgrade. On Pro and Business, you can top up or upgrade. On Enterprise, limits are set in the contract and are never a hard stop.
- **Changing plans** — possible at any time. Nothing already built is lost when you switch.

---

## Which plan fits

- **Starter** — one assistant, basic models, small allowance. For exploring the product.
- **Pro** — all models, your own AI key, memory, data connectors and images. For individuals doing real work.
- **Business** — a pooled allowance for a team, seats and roles, shared knowledge, approvals and audit. For teams.
- **Enterprise** — negotiated limits, SSO, private deployment, dedicated capacity and longest retention. For regulated or large organisations.
